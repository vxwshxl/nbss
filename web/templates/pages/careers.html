{{ template "eyebrow" dict "Num" "03" "Text" "How to apply" }}

Three steps, and nobody asks you for money.

NBSS never charges a fee for a job, training, a uniform or a "placement". If anyone asks you for money in our name, call {{ .Site.Phone }} and tell us.

  1. 01

    Apply online or walk in

    Use the form on any vacancy page, or come to the Kokrajhar office between 09:30 and 18:00, Monday to Saturday.

  2. 02

    Verification interview

    HR shortlists weekly. Bring Aadhaar, address proof and two references with working phone numbers.

  3. 03

    Induction, then posting

    Twenty-one paid days at the training ground, a supervised attachment, then your first independent posting.
