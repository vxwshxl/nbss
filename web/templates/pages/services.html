Four steps, roughly ten days.

  1. 01

    You call or send the form

    Tell us the site, the shift pattern and roughly how many people you have in mind. A rough answer is fine — that is what the survey is for.

  2. 02

    A field officer walks the site

    Within 48 hours inside the BTR districts. We look at entries, blind spots, lighting, the rest facility and where the register will live.

  3. 03

    A costed proposal, built up line by line

    Wage, EPF, ESI, bonus, leave, uniform, service charge and GST shown separately. If a number looks odd, ask — it will have a reason.

  4. 04

    Deployment and a two-week review

    Guards on site within seven working days of signing, then a review at day fourteen to fix whatever the site survey did not anticipate.
