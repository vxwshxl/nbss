{{ template "eyebrow" dict "Num" "01" "Text" "The premise" }}

Most guarding failures are training failures.

A guard who lets a truck out because the paperwork looked roughly right has not been dishonest — he has been untrained. A guard who freezes at a fire alarm has not been careless. A guard who argues with an agitated attendant in a casualty ward has not been rude. In each case somebody skipped a module.

So we do not deploy on the strength of an interview. Every recruit, fresher or twenty-year veteran, goes through the same induction at the Kokrajhar training ground and is signed off by a field officer before independent posting. It is paid time, which is why we take it seriously and so do they.

  • {{ template "icon" "check" }}Paid from day one — nobody trains hungry
  • {{ template "icon" "check" }}Uniform, boots and ID issued on completion, not before
  • {{ template "icon" "check" }}Written and practical assessment at the end of each module
  • {{ template "icon" "check" }}Quarterly refresher for every person on the roll
  • {{ template "icon" "check" }}Site-specific briefing before every new posting

{{ template "eyebrow" dict "Num" "03" "Text" "Before the uniform" }}

Verification runs in parallel, and it is the harder gate.

01

Identity

Aadhaar and permanent address verified against documents, not against a photocopy handed over at interview.

02

Police clearance

Requested from the home police station of the candidate's permanent address, not the district they applied in.

03

References

Two independent references contacted by an HR officer by phone. A reference nobody can reach is not a reference.

04

Medical fitness

Basic fitness, vision and blood pressure. Night-shift and armed postings carry a stricter standard.

05

Re-verification

The whole file is refreshed every two years for every person still on the roll.

06

Enhanced check

School, hostel and hospital postings carry an additional check before a candidate is placed on that site.
